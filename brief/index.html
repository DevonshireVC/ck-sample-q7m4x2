Case Knowledge: A 90-Day Pilot

A brief for your review and approval, from Dr. Aaron Smiley

Thank you for helping shape this. Here is the idea we talked about, in one place, so you can look it over and let me know if it has your approval.

What it is

A 90-day pilot, sponsored by Elanco (already funded), for VetCor's veterinary leadership team, a pilot group of about 50 veterinarians. Our goal is simple: encouragement and education that support our doctors and the patients they care for. Veterinarian engagement is key.

How it works

  • Each veterinarian gets a daily email built from their own VetSOAP exam-room transcripts.
  • It reviews the cases they saw on the previous workday (Monday's email covers Friday). Each case is labeled with the real day it was seen.
  • It offers 2 to 3 teaching themes, each with an open, trustworthy article link and a short note on why it matters for that case.
  • It takes about 5 to 8 minutes to read.

How we keep it caring and honest

  • The email is AI-prepared and says so plainly. I review flagged cards for accuracy. No human signs it, and it does not promise replies to flags.
  • Each email includes a pause option and a Friday reflection. There are no streaks or scorecards.
  • Recipients are told that other VetCor leaders get the same daily email, so it feels like belonging, not comparison.
  • Elanco sees only anonymized case knowledge, never individual doctors' cases or names.
  • No RACE CE credit is claimed.
